Singapore buildings 3D Tiles from OneMap 3D

A demo of Singapore buildings 3D tiles from OneMap 3D, rendered with Three.js.

Drag with one finger to pan, pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers side by side to tilt or twist them to rotate. With a mouse: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and Ctrl/⌘-drag up and down to tilt, left and right to rotate.

WARNING: This demo streams 3D tiles and uses a lot of bandwidth. Expect roughly 10 MB when the view first loads, and more as you pan and zoom around the city.
